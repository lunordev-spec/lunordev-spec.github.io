About Advora

Advora is a specialized social platform for students. Unlike traditional social media, Advora focuses on academic growth, peer-to-peer learning, and resource sharing without the distractions.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Advora collects minimal data to function as a social platform.

  • Account Info: We store your username and educational details to connect you with relevant peers.
  • Content: Notes and posts shared on Advora are stored securely.
  • Security: We use industry-standard encryption (Lunor Tech DataX Shield standards) to protect your identity.

Terms of Service

By joining Advora, you agree to maintain a respectful academic environment.

  • Harassment or bullying is strictly prohibited.
  • Academic dishonesty (cheating) facilitation is not allowed.
  • Users are responsible for the content they upload.
